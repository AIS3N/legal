Privacy Policy for myTasbih

Last updated: April 26, 2026

myTasbih is a free Islamic dhikr counter app. This privacy policy explains our data practices.

Data Collection
myTasbih does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data (your count and goal) is stored locally on your device only.

Third Parties
myTasbih contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
